Are drying times changing or taking several cycles?

For a dryer-vent project in Forest, note how often the dryer runs, whether loads take longer than usual, and where the exterior termination is located. Photograph the vent route if it is visible, including bends, long sections, and the outside hood. Questions About Your Forest Request

What details help with a Forest quote?

Ask how the vacuum connects to the trunk lines and how each supply and return run will be brushed while suction is maintained. This combination is intended to carry loosened material out through the collection hose rather than release it into rooms. Confirm which branches are accessible and included, since a partial scope cannot describe the entire system. Ask which duct sections can be photographed before and after, and whether the images will be reviewed with you. Photos help compare the same accessible areas and clarify what was reached; they do not prove that every hidden surface is clean. Record any branches or components that could not be inspected so the limits are clear.
